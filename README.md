I'm a software developer with a strong background in blockchain infrastructure, InfoSec, and full-stack engineering. I specialize in the **Solana and Sui ecosystems** - building DeFi protocols, mobile dApps, developer tooling and injection frameworks - and in **self-custodial wallets and stablecoin payment infrastructure** (Tether WDK). I design and ship full-stack AI products end to end. I hold a postgraduate-equivalent certification in **Cybersecurity & Data Protection (DPO / GDPR)**, bridging the technical and compliance sides of modern software.

I like going deep: investigating how things work, taking them apart, and building things that hold up. Currently focused on blockchain developer tooling, stablecoin payment rails (Tether WDK), autonomous AI agent systems, and cross-chain infrastructure.

> **💼 Available for development.** Most products showcased here are working prototypes - the complete product, including the private production backend, can be built and delivered for you under a development agreement. **Get in touch on [X / Twitter](https://x.com/franny_dustmast).**
